The Laws

Your words become the world. The outcome is not for sale.
Everything that decides what happens is on this page. The AI only translates your words into what a thing is made of; these laws decide the rest. They were published before the run and never change — not by a donation, not by a vote, not by the operator.

Read it as: fire on wood → it burns. Ice on flesh → it dies. Electricity on metal → it is electrified.

RULE 2 — ELEMENT CHANGES ELEMENT

  • water + fire → the fire goes out
  • fire + ice → the ice becomes water
  • ice + water → the water freezes (while a blizzard lasts)
  • wind + fire → the fire jumps two tiles downwind
  • electricity + water → anything standing in it is shocked; flesh dies

RULE 3 — MATERIAL DOES NOT CHANGE MATERIAL

Nothing. Stone never changes wood. The land is quiet until an element arrives. That is why fire, water and weather are the only things that make history here.

SUPPORT

  • Nothing stands on water.
  • Ash holds nothing tall.
  • Things that need water (a mill, a dock, a boat, a rice paddy) must sit beside it.

LIFE

People come to a shelter when there is food within 6 tiles and, at night, light within 3 tiles. They leave when the food is gone. Fire, ice, lightning and flood drive them out.

  • People fight fire. A burning thing within 4 tiles of a shelter where people live, and within 4 tiles of water, is put out by them at the end of the hour — it takes 2 people. A fire that has just started gets one hour before they reach it.

GROWTH

  • Ash returns to earth on its own; rain turns it back at once.
  • Grass sprouts on bare earth, becomes a young tree after 72 hours and an old tree after 240.
  • Only grass and trees come on their own. They feed and shelter nobody. Fields, houses and lights come only from words.
  • Wild plants make way when something is built on them.
  • At most 15% of the land is wild at once.

BEASTS

  • Where grass covers 8% of the land, wild animals come (deer, hare, boar) — at most 2.5% of the tiles.
  • They eat grass, run from fire, and move on after 48 hours without grazing.
  • They never eat each other. That would be material changing material, and RULE 3 forbids it.
  • They feed and shelter nobody. A herd you make with words stays where you put it.

FORM — HOW A THING IS DRAWN

A thing is drawn as one of these: house, hall, tower, keep, dome, gate, wall, bridge, statue, monument, well, mill, boat, tent, field, tree, animal, lamp, pyramid, ring, block.

The form is appearance only. It changes nothing about what happens. A stone tower and a wooden tower look alike; only the wooden one burns. The AI reads the form from your words the same way it reads the material — it never decides the outcome.

Ask for anything and it appears. If a word is not in any list — an invented creature, a word in a language we did not anticipate — it still appears: its colour, its body, the pitch of its roof, the number of its bays are derived from the word itself, so the same word always makes the same thing. Living things are built from traits read out of the name (wings, horns, fins, a long neck, six legs). What the laws above do to it is decided by its material, never by its name. Things built by a banner's members fly its colour.

LIMITS

  • One word per person per tick. The rest wait for the next one.
  • At most 161 built things stand at once (28% of the land). When the land is full, something must fall first.
  • Small elements a viewer may place — a bonfire, a bucket of water, a block of ice — last 6 ticks. Storms, lightning, floods and blizzards are not for sale; they come only from the table below.
  • When three or more people ask for the same thing within one hour, it rises at full size — 3 wide, 3 tall. Consensus builds big, never strong — its material still decides everything that happens to it.

CORRECTION

The one who made a thing may correct its material, form or place once, within the next hour, by saying "no, stone", "no, a tower" or "no, I4". Nothing else about it changes, and nobody else can. The AI never judges this — it only reads the word after "no" and the published rule does the rest.

TIME

The world advances one hour every 60 seconds. Words are read every 15 seconds - a thing you ask for stands within a quarter of an hour, and burns, floods and grows on the hour.

24 ticks a day (one tick = one hour of the world). Night is 20:00–06:00. A season is 420 days — about a week of real time. When it ends the land is sealed and a new one begins. The chronicle is append-only: territory is lost, history is not.

WHAT GOES IN THE PERMANENT RECORD

No name may claim to stand above the laws. absolute, invincible, invulnerable, indestructible, unbreakable, unburnable, fireproof, flameproof and the same words in other languages are refused — "the unburnable house" would become a lie the moment it burned, and the chronicle holds no lies. A name never changes what happens; a name that says otherwise is not written down.

Names and inscriptions are checked before they are written, by a published list — not by an AI. A name is at most 24 characters, an inscription 40. Slurs, self-harm, impersonation, spam and sexual content are refused; look-alike spellings are folded together first. The gate's hash is 15cc1a4c6f0e1313.

The AI is a translator, not a judge. It reads only what a thing is made of — wood, stone, metal, flesh, plant, cloth, glass — and where you meant to put it. Everything else is above.
